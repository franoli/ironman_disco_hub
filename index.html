IRONMAN Mobile App

Official mobile companion for athletes worldwide

Executive Summary

We are launching the official IRONMAN Mobile App. While athletes are the primary audience, IRONMAN equally values the experience of fans, spectators, and partners who are essential to the IRONMAN ecosystem. The app will serve all these stakeholders with tailored experiences.

The mobile app will consolidate critical athlete services previously scattered across multiple platforms. The goal is to reduce friction across the athlete's journey, from registration through race day and beyond, while introducing new opportunities for loyalty, cross-sell, and engagement.

This app will integrate with existing systems, external timing and registration partners, and customer support & commerce tools to deliver a scalable foundation for innovation, personalization, and operational efficiency.

Vision & Goals

Strategic direction and success criteria from PRD

📄 View PRD Source Document →

✓ Goals

Best-in-Class Mobile App

Create a mobile app for IRONMAN athletes that centralizes event discovery, registration, profile management, race tracking, and post-race engagement.

Personalized Experience

Deliver a personalized athlete experience by leveraging athlete history, milestones, and performance data to tailor content and recommendations.

Support Entire Race Journey

From onboarding and account creation to race day execution and post-event highlights, enabling athletes to plan, perform, and reflect seamlessly.

Live Tracking for Supporters

Enable live tracking and race visibility for friends, family, and spectators during events to extend the IRONMAN experience beyond the athlete.

Scalable Multi-Brand Platform

Build a platform that can support multiple IRONMAN brands (RnR, Epic, etc.) and evolve with future business needs.

Drive Engagement & Cross-sell

Surface relevant events, merchandise, and content based on athlete preferences and behaviors.

Reduce Support Tickets

Improve self-service via a centralized hub for waivers, deferrals, transfers, and other race services.

✗ Non-Goals (Out of Scope)

✗

Not a Platform Replacement

The app is not intended to replace core registration platforms or timing systems; it will integrate with them.

✗

Not Fan-Only

This is not a fan-only app. The primary user is the athlete, though we will support spectator experiences via features like athlete tracking.

✗

Not for Event Staff/Volunteers

This release is not focused on event staff/volunteer tooling (though future phases may explore operational modules).

✗

No Full E-commerce in V1

The app will not offer full merchandising or e-commerce functionality in v1; product linking will redirect to existing stores.

THE ELITE COMPETITOR

Disciplined, data-driven athletes who strive for mastery, podium finishes and recognition

👤 Who are they?

The performance minded IRONMAN athlete chasing personal bests, competing for age group rankings and world championship slots. For these A-type personalities, IRONMAN racing is a way of life and they measure success in data, discipline and results.

🎯 What motivates them?

These individuals care deeply about performance improvement and focus heavily on data to drive optimization opportunities in their training. Recognition within the triathlon community matters - they chase AWA status, world championship slots and age group rankings.

💬 How should we talk to them?

Tone and content should focus on prestige, performance optimization and technical training/gear topics.

Voice & Tone

Expert, technical, performance-driven

Audience Motivation

Mastery, optimization, recognition

✈️

DESTINATION ADVENTURER

Travel-focused athletes who value new experiences, scenic courses and storytelling

👤 Who are they?

These athletes see each race as an experience and a chance to explore something new. The opportunity to travel, explore and storytelling is just as important as their athletic achievements.

🎯 What motivates them?

These athletes thrive on adventure, discovery and the reward of racing in amazing new destinations. These are often working professionals who integrate IRONMAN racing into their lifestyle and leisure plans.

💬 How should we talk to them?

Inspirational storytelling that focuses on the destination, the experience and cultural opportunities. Discovery, memories and storytelling potential are key.

Voice & Tone

Inspirational, sensory, emotional storytelling

Audience Motivation

Travel, experience, discovery

💪

EVERYDAY ACHIEVER

Dedicated age group athletes balancing work, family and training

👤 Who are they?

Hardworking, disciplined individuals balancing training with busy jobs, family and everyday life. They are not the fastest and not chasing championship slots but are loyal athletes that take pride in progress and personal bests. Time is scarce.

🎯 What motivates them?

They seek personal growth, structure and balance. The training and race day experience gives them confidence and identity beyond their family.

💬 How should we talk to them?

Motivational and relatable content. Time is at a premium so content relating to time hacks, balance and perseverance is valued.

Voice & Tone

Motivational, relatable, practical

Audience Motivation

Balance, structure, personal growth

⭐

BUCKET LIST BELIEVER

First-timers chasing a once-in-a-lifetime goal to prove Anything Is Possible

👤 Who are they?

First-timers or casual endurance athletes inspired by the IRONMAN brand's mystique. Often motivated by a personal milestone, challenge or inspirational story. Becoming an IRONMAN is a life goal.

🎯 What motivates them?

Transformation, self-belief and proving they can do something extraordinary. The "Anything Is Possible" promise resonates deeply. The finish line and title of IRONMAN is the ultimate motivator.

💬 How should we talk to them?

Inspirational brand messaging ("Anything is Possible", "Become One") and relatable athlete stories. Encouraging, belief-building, removal of fear/barriers.

Voice & Tone

Emotional, reassuring, empowering

Audience Motivation

Transformation, confidence, inspiration

🤝

CLUB CONNECTOR

Community focused athletes motivated by camaraderie and teamwork

👤 Who are they?

Socially driven individuals who thrive in community. Club leaders, members or social group athletes who value belonging as much as performance. The cultural heartbeat of local triathlon scenes.

🎯 What motivates them?

Fueled by camaraderie, teamwork and shared achievements. Recognition, social connection and helping others succeed are central to their motivation.

💬 How should we talk to them?

Focused on inclusivity, community and club identity. Shared achievements and community connection are key pillars.

Voice & Tone

Inclusive, energetic, celebratory

Audience Motivation

Belonging, camaraderie, teamwork

SUPPORTIVE SPECTATOR

Family and friends who travel to support their athlete

👤 Who are they?

Spouses, children, parents, and close friends of IRONMAN athletes who make the trip to cheer from the sidelines. Their weekend revolves around their athlete, but they also seek memorable, hassle-free experiences of their own.

🎯 What motivates them?

Emotionally invested in their athlete's journey and want to feel included and empowered to support them. They value live updates, clear maps, and spectator tools to plan their day. Also drawn to VIP passes, IRONKIDS events, or celebratory moments post-race.

💬 How should we talk to them?

Supportive, inclusive, informative, and centered on helping them navigate race weekend while celebrating their role in the athlete's journey.

Voice & Tone

Empathetic, informative, inclusive

Motivation

Support, involvement, memorable experiences

REMOTE RACE FOLLOWER

Digitally engaged fans who follow events remotely

👤 Who are they?

Fans of the sport — from family back home to diehard IRONMAN followers. They tune in from afar to follow friends, favorite pros, or the thrill of competition. They consume IRONMAN via the app, livestreams, and social media.

🎯 What motivates them?

They seek connection, access, and drama. They want fast and reliable tracking, access to pro splits and commentary, and immersive media that makes them feel part of the action even from a distance.

💬 How should we talk to them?

Content should be fast, digestible, and emotionally resonant. Think live alerts, pro storylines, Instagram-ready moments, and quick ways to follow and cheer for athletes.

Voice & Tone

Timely, energetic, engaging

Motivation

Connection, access, entertainment

🔐 Authentication Architecture (Azure AD B2C)

⚠️ Important: Azure AD B2C will no longer be available for new customers after May 1, 2025. Consider Microsoft Entra External ID for future implementations.

Implementation Approach

  • • Library: react-native-app-auth with Azure AD B2C provider
  • • Issuer URL: https://<tenant>.b2clogin.com/<tenant>.onmicrosoft.com/<user_flow>/v2.0
  • • Scopes: openid, offline_access
  • • Token Handling: Bearer tokens in Authorization header

User Flows Required

  • • susi: Combined Sign-in/Sign-up flow
  • • edit_profile: Profile editing flow
  • • reset_password: Password reset flow

No Social Login: Per PRD, email/password only to avoid duplicate account issues

RTRT.me - Real-Time Race Tracking

Live tracking, split times, and athlete location

API Specifications

  • • Protocol: JSON + REST (HTTPS)
  • • Live Data: TCP connections for real-time feeds
  • • Authentication: Application ID in query string
  • • Endpoints: profiles, splits, locations

Key Features

  • • Real-time athlete position ('loc' object with coords)
  • • Estimated miles/percentage ('emiles', 'epc')
  • • Split versioning ('_ver' for cache invalidation)
  • • Checkpoint timing data

GET https://api.rtrt.me/events/{eventId}/participants/{bibNumber}/splits?appId={YOUR_APP_ID}

📍 RTRT.me Integration Context

RTRT.me provides both REST and TCP APIs for race tracking. REST API is rate-limited (120 requests/minute). TCP streaming is recommended for real-time updates. Data includes athlete positions, split times, rankings, and estimated finish times. Ensure we understand the authentication model and data refresh rates.

💡 Strategic Insight: Identity-Based Loyalty

Traditional loyalty programs reward transactions. IRONMAN has a unique opportunity to build identity-based loyalty—where being part of the IRONMAN tribe is the reward itself. The M-dot tattoo phenomenon proves athletes already self-identify with the brand at a deep level.

💼 Strategic Business Case for Wearables Integration

✅ Why Integrate Wearables?

  • Data Richness: Training history enables meaningful personalization (race readiness scores, taper recommendations)
  • User Stickiness: Connected athletes are 3x more likely to return weekly
  • Reduced Friction: Auto-import race results to Strava = viral sharing
  • Competitive Necessity: Strava Connect is table stakes in endurance apps
  • Premium Features: AI coaching and readiness scores require health data

⚠️ Strategic Considerations

  • Not a Training App: IRONMAN shouldn't compete with Strava/TrainingPeaks for daily use
  • Partner Relationships: Strava partnership could enable co-marketing opportunities
  • Data Privacy: Health data requires explicit consent and GDPR/HIPAA compliance
  • API Costs: Some integrations (Garmin Health API) have per-user licensing fees
  • Maintenance Burden: Third-party APIs break—need monitoring and versioning strategy

🎯 Core Value Proposition

The IRONMAN app should be the race layer that sits on top of athletes' existing training ecosystems. We enhance the race experience without forcing athletes to change their daily habits. Import their training data, enrich it with IRONMAN context, and export race achievements back to their preferred platforms.

Garmin Health API requires a partnership agreement and may have per-active-user licensing fees (~$0.10-0.30/user/month). For 100K connected users, this could be $10-30K/month. Consider starting with the free Connect API for activities only, then upgrade to Health API when value is proven.

🍎

Apple Health / HealthKit

On-device health data hub for iOS users

MVP CRITICAL
📥 Data Types Available
  • • Workouts (all types, with route data)
  • • Heart rate (continuous + zones)
  • • Sleep analysis (stages available)
  • • HRV (Heart Rate Variability)
  • • Active/Resting energy
  • • Step count and distance
🔄 Unique Benefits
  • • Aggregates data from ALL connected apps/devices
  • • No API costs or rate limits
  • • Data stays on device (privacy+)
  • • Background fetch available
  • • Includes Apple Watch data automatically
🔧 Technical Details
  • Framework: HealthKit
  • Auth: On-device permission prompt
  • Storage: Local (no cloud sync)
  • Updates: Background delivery available
✅ Pros
  • • FREE - no API costs
  • • Privacy-focused (Apple compliance)
  • • Aggregates all iOS health data
  • • Simple permission flow
⚠️ Considerations
  • • iOS only (need Android equivalent)
  • • Data not synced across devices
  • • User must explicitly grant each type
  • • App Store review scrutinizes usage
📱 Implementation Note

HealthKit requires clear usage descriptions in Info.plist. Apple reviews health data access carefully—be prepared to justify each data type. Request only what you need for MVP; add more types later as features require them.

🤖

Google Health Connect

Android's unified health data platform

MVP CRITICAL
📥 Comparable to Apple Health

Health Connect provides the Android equivalent of HealthKit, aggregating data from Samsung Health, Fitbit, Garmin, and other apps.

  • • Exercise sessions with routes
  • • Heart rate and HRV
  • • Sleep stages
  • • Steps, distance, calories
✅ Key Advantages
  • • FREE - no licensing costs
  • • Standardized API (replacing Google Fit)
  • • Required by Samsung Health, Fitbit
  • • Permission UI standardized by Google
  • • Background sync available
⚠️ Platform Maturity Note

Health Connect is relatively new (2022) and still maturing. Not all Android devices have it pre-installed—users may need to download from Play Store. Adoption is growing but not universal. Test across device manufacturers (Samsung, Pixel, OnePlus).
